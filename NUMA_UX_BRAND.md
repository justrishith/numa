# NUMA UX and Brand

> Canonical source of truth for NUMA's visual identity, interface, and interaction design.

**Status:** Active
**Owner:** NUMA project

## Brand concept

NUMA is the Electric Night Butterfly: a luminous intelligence moving through a dark digital laboratory.

It should feel:

- Beautiful
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Precise
- Futuristic
- Personal
- Trustworthy

It must not feel like a generic chatbot, admin dashboard, cartoon assistant, or fake sci-fi control panel.

## Butterfly design

- Dark translucent wings
- Violet and cyan edge lighting
- Luminous central core
- Circuit/neural wing patterns
- Subtle N-shaped symmetry
- Refined geometric form
- High-quality 3D dashboard asset
- Lightweight vector/native variant for the notch

## Butterfly states

```text
Idle: folded wings and slow breathing
Listening: wings open and antennae pulse
Transcribing: waveform energy travels through the wings
Thinking: wing patterns shimmer
Executing: butterfly flies along a light path
Approval: butterfly pauses at a luminous boundary
Verifying: butterfly hovers and scans
Success: butterfly lands and pulses
Failure: wings fold and lighting turns amber
Stopped: wings close and motion freezes
```

Animations must represent real NUMA state. No fake success or theatrical thinking while no work is happening.

## Product surfaces

### Poni notch

Ambient status, voice entry, current task, approval prompts, and emergency stop.

### Command center

Current focus, active project, tasks, routines, lab status, recent decisions, suggestions, and the butterfly.

### Conversation

Messages, tool cards, progress, approvals, verification results, retries, files, and artifacts.

### Projects

Goals, tasks, files, decisions, experiments, activity, and linked knowledge.

### Lab

Experiments, processes, logs, measurements, artifacts, and reproducible runs.

### Memory

Inspectable preferences, entities, decisions, project context, sources, confidence, editing, and deletion.

### Activity

An honest timeline of requests, tools, approvals, observations, verification, recovery, and completion.

## UX principles

1. Progressive disclosure instead of clutter.
2. Every long-running action has visible progress.
3. Every failure explains what happened and offers recovery.
4. Critical actions are previewed before submission.
5. The dashboard is a real stateful application, not static HTML.
6. Keyboard, voice, mouse, and touch interactions should coexist.
7. Motion is purposeful, graceful, and accessible.
8. Reduce or disable motion when requested.
